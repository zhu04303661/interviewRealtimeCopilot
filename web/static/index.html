Why people use it

Four problems it solves on interview day

Interview in English without the language barrier

Every question is translated into Chinese the moment it is asked, and each hint gives you natural English you can say straight away.

An expert listening in, answering with real knowledge

The copilot follows the conversation and answers technical questions with precise domain knowledge — mechanisms, trade-offs, numbers — combined with your own experience.

No time to prepare? Start from what you have

Drop in your resume, notes or the job description. Questions are extracted and practised out loud in minutes, and every live hint is drawn from those files.

Coding questions solved from your screen

When a coding problem appears on your screen, the copilot reads it and writes a working solution with its complexity and edge cases — hidden from screen share.

Built for real interview moments

The six moments it handles for you

From the first video call to the final debrief — wherever an interview gets hard, the copilot has a specific answer.

Video interviews on Zoom, Teams or Meet

The copilot hears the meeting and your microphone separately, recognises each question once the interviewer finishes, and streams a one-minute answer built from your resume — while you keep eye contact.

Interviewing in your second language

Every interviewer line is translated as it is spoken (English ↔ Chinese), and hints come in both languages, so you understand precisely and answer fluently.

Technical and coding rounds

Press ⌥S — or let auto-capture do it — and the copilot reads the coding problem, diagram or error on your screen and explains a solution with its trade-offs.

Rehearsing your prepared Q&A

Upload your preparation document and every prepared question is extracted. A spoken AI interviewer asks them in order, at random or improvises, follows up once, and never repeats itself.

Getting stuck mid-answer

Already talking? Expand answer continues from what you just said with the most valuable facts you have not mentioned yet, in under a minute.

Improving after every interview

Each session ends with a debrief: every question reviewed, what was missing, a stronger spoken version — and the recording to replay.

How it works

Go Live

Privacy

Your preparation never leaves your Mac.

  • Goals, documents, transcripts, debriefs and recordings are stored locally.
  • Audio is processed only during a session you start.
  • The cockpit is excluded from screen sharing and recordings.

Answers only from what is true

Hints use facts from your resume and notes. When something is not in your materials, the copilot tells you how to answer honestly instead of inventing experience.
