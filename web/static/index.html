What it does · 功能

Everything you need during the interview — and before it.

🎧

Live bilingual transcript

Hears the meeting audio and your microphone separately, and translates each line between English and Chinese. 会议声音和你的声音分开转写，中英对照。

❓

Question detection

Recognises when the interviewer has finished asking — including questions spread over pauses — and turns it into a clear question card. 识别完整问题，包括中间有停顿的长问题。

💡

Grounded answer hints

Three or four speakable bullets in under a minute, using only facts from your resume and notes — never invented experience. 一分钟内可说完的要点，只引用你的真实经历。

🗣️

Spoken practice interviews

An AI interviewer asks your prepared questions out loud, follows up once, and lets you jump, shuffle or improvise. AI 面试官用语音提问，可按题库顺序、随机或临场发挥。

🖥️

Solve the screen

One shortcut reads a coding problem, diagram or error on your screen and explains the answer. 一键读取屏幕上的编程题、图表或报错并给出解答。

📈

Debrief after every session

Every question reviewed with what was missing and a stronger spoken version, plus the recording to replay. 逐题复盘，并保留录音可回放。

How it works · 使用方式

Ready in four steps.

Create a goal — the role and company, plus the job description. 创建目标职位。
Add your resume and notes — PDF, Word, Markdown, up to 500 MB; every prepared Q&A is extracted. 上传简历和准备资料。
Practise out loud — the AI interviewer asks your prepared questions. 先做语音练习。
Go Live — open your meeting app and let the copilot listen. 正式面试时开启实时模式。

Private by design · 隐私优先

Documents, transcripts, debriefs and recordings are stored on your computer. Audio is sent only to the speech and language models that produce your transcript and hints, and only during a session you start. The cockpit can be hidden from screen sharing. 资料、记录和录音都在本机；只在你开始会话时把音频发送给语音和语言模型。
