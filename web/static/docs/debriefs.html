History and debriefs

Debriefs and recordings

Every session is recorded on your Mac and reviewed question by question.

  • History lists your sessions; open one to read its debrief and play the recording.
  • The debrief reviews every question (up to 40 per session): what was missing and a stronger spoken version, using only what you actually said.
  • The recording mixes your microphone, the computer audio and the practice interviewer's voice. It is saved when you click End.
No debrief? A session needs some of your own speech to review. If the microphone was muted or denied, there is nothing to assess.
