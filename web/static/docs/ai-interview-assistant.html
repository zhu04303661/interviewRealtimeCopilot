What is an AI interview assistant, and how do you choose one?

What these tools do during a live interview, what to check before trusting one, and where they do not help.

An AI interview assistant — some people call it an interview copilot, 面试神器 or 面试外挂 — is an app that listens to your interview, recognises the question and suggests an answer while you are still on the call.

What to check

QuestionWhy it matters
Does it answer from your own resume and notes?Generic answers are easy to spot; invented experience fails follow-up questions.
How fast is the first hint?More than a few seconds and the silence is noticeable.
Does it understand questions with pauses?Real interviewers think aloud; a tool that splits one question into three gives three wrong answers.
Is it hidden from screen sharing?Otherwise the interviewer sees the window.
Can you rehearse with it?The first time you use it should not be the real interview.
Where is your data kept?Your resume and recordings are personal; local storage is safer.

Where it does not help

  • It cannot give you experience you do not have — and a good tool will not pretend to.
  • Check the rules of each interview and use it within them.

Interview Realtime Copilot is built around these points: answers from your own materials, question detection that survives pauses, English–Chinese translation, spoken practice, and local storage.
