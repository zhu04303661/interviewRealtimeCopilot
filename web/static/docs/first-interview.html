Getting started

Your first interview, end to end

From an empty app to a debrief in about fifteen minutes.

  1. Create a goal: role, company and (ideally) the job description.
  2. Add your resume and preparation notes on the goal's Prep tab. Wait until they show Ready.
  3. Click Practice. In Preflight choose the practice mode and sources, test the microphone, and start.
  4. Answer out loud. Use Suggest answer when you are stuck, or Expand answer to continue what you said.
  5. Click End. The debrief appears in History within a minute.
  6. On interview day, open your meeting app, click Go Live and keep the cockpit beside the call.
