Getting started

What Interview Realtime Copilot does, the loop it runs on, and how to find your way around this guide.

Interview Realtime Copilot is a Mac app that helps you through real job interviews. It listens to the interviewer, translates as they speak, recognises each question and shows a short answer built from your own resume and notes. It also rehearses with you before the interview and reviews every session afterwards.

New here? Install the app, sign in with your website account, create a goal and add your resume — then start a practice session. The realtime AI engine is ready as soon as you sign in; every plan includes a monthly allowance.

The loop: Prepare, Practise, Perform, Review

Everything in the app follows the same four steps for each job you apply for. Go round the loop once per interview.

1. Prepare

Create a goal (role and company) and add your resume and preparation notes.

2. Practise

A spoken AI interviewer asks your prepared questions and follows up.

3. Perform

Go Live in your real interview; the cockpit listens, translates and hints.

4. Review

Read the debrief and replay the recording, then prepare better next time.

Words you will see a lot

TermMeaning
GoalOne role at one company. Practice, live sessions and debriefs belong to a goal.
Knowledge sourcesThe resume and documents a session may use to answer.
CockpitThe floating window you see during a session, hidden from screen sharing.
HintThree or four speakable bullets in English and Chinese.
DebriefThe review written after each session.
AI allowanceThe monthly AI usage included in your plan.

Common questions

  • Which Macs are supported? Apple Silicon (M1–M4) with macOS 14.2 or later.
  • Does the interviewer see it? No — the cockpit is excluded from screen sharing and recordings.
