Troubleshooting

Fixes for the problems people meet most often.

ProblemWhat to do
The Computer meter does not move in LiveAllow System Audio Recording for the app, quit and reopen it, and check the meeting's sound plays through this Mac.
The Mic meter does not moveAllow the microphone in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone; check the input device in macOS Sound settings.
“Your monthly AI allowance is used up”Subscribe in Settings → Account, or wait for next month's allowance.
macOS says the app cannot be openedSystem Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway (preview builds are not notarised yet).
Cannot reach the account serviceCheck your internet connection or proxy; the app keeps you signed in and retries.
A continuous question is split into piecesUsually a very long pause; the copilot still answers the combined question. Tell us if it keeps happening.

Contact support

Email linkyourfuture2026@outlook.com with what you did, what you expected and what happened. In the app, the Help button (?) opens a new email for you.
