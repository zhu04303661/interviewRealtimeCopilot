Privacy · 隐私

Interview Realtime Copilot is built to keep your preparation private. 我们以隐私优先设计产品。

On your computer · 保存在本机

Goals, documents, extracted questions, transcripts, debriefs and recordings are stored locally in the app's data folder.

Sent for processing · 处理时发送

During a session you start, audio and the text needed to answer (the question and relevant passages of your materials) are sent to the speech and language models that produce transcripts and hints. Nothing is sent when no session is running.

This website · 本网站

We store your email, a password hash and your subscription status. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card number.
