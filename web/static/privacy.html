Privacy

Stored on your Mac

Goals, documents, extracted questions, transcripts, debriefs and recordings are kept in the app's local data folder.

Sent for processing

During a session you start, audio and the text needed to answer — the question and relevant passages of your materials — are processed by our AI engine, which runs on our servers and on trusted AI processing partners bound by data-protection terms. Nothing is sent when no session is running.

This website

We store your email, a password hash and your subscription status. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see your card number.
