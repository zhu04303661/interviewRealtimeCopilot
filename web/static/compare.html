Where we fit

What is actually different here

The only one built for English–Chinese interviews

Every interviewer line is translated as it is spoken and every hint comes in both languages. Built for people whose experience is in Chinese but whose interview is in English.

A third of the price of the nearest rival

A free monthly allowance on every account, then £25 a month — Linkjob and Interview Coder charge three to twelve times that. A China site (copilot.xichigroup.com.cn) serves pages and downloads directly for mainland users.

Practice and debrief in the same app

A spoken AI interviewer rehearses your prepared questions before the real call, and every session ends with a question-by-question debrief and recording — most tools only cover the live moment.

Where rivals are ahead

We are a newer, smaller product: no Linux or mobile app, no phone-call mode, a smaller community and less public testing. If you interview on a phone or Linux today, look at Linkjob first.

How to choose

Five checks before you pay anyone

  • Does it answer from your own resume and notes? Generic answers fail follow-up questions.
  • Try the free option on a mock call first — never discover the product during the real interview.
  • Check it stays hidden from screen sharing in your meeting app, with your own recording.
  • Compare the effective monthly price after tax and annual lock-ins, not the headline number.
  • No tool is 100% undetectable — anyone claiming that is marketing. Check the interview's rules and use it within them.

Try before you compare

Download it, run the free spoken practice and watch the live translation. If a rival's free tier does the same job better for you, use it.
